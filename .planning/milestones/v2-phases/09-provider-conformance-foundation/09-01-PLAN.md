## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Upstream outcome → redispatch policy | Untrusted transport/status/event evidence can trigger a billable or side-effecting duplicate request. |
| Provider event → client commitment | Tool activity can become replay-unsafe before ordinary text is visible. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-09-02 | Repudiation | WebSocket/HTTP hit accounting | medium | mitigate | Exact mock hit counts prove whether a second request occurred without logging sensitive bodies. |
| T-09-03 | Denial of Service | bounded retry/failover loops | medium | mitigate | Preserve existing retry budgets, route counts, and pre-response-only eligibility. |
</threat_model>
