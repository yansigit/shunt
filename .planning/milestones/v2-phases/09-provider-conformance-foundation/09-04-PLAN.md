<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Inbound client headers → provider request | Attacker-controlled credential slots must not override gateway-owned authentication. |
| Gateway credential → configured origin | A route-selected secret must not cross to another origin during failover. |
| Response body → shared capacity | A stalled or abandoned client can retain upstream work and permits. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-09-12 | Spoofing | provider-owned auth injection | high | mitigate | Exact and absent header matchers prove inbound slots are removed and only selected auth is injected. |
| T-09-13 | Information Disclosure | `Credential` diagnostics | high | mitigate | Manual redacted formatting and fragment-absence tests prevent secret disclosure. |
| T-09-14 | Elevation of Privilege | cross-origin failover | high | mitigate | Per-attempt credential resolution and destination-specific absence assertions prevent credential replay. |
| T-09-15 | Denial of Service | response-owned permits/admission | medium | mitigate | Observable cancellation plus bounded capacity-reacquisition tests prove RAII cleanup. |
</threat_model>
